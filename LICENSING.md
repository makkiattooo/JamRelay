The complete license text is available in `LICENSE`.

`AGPL-3.0-only` permits commercial and non-commercial use, modification, and redistribution subject to its conditions.

Among other requirements, modified versions used to provide functionality to users over a computer network may trigger an obligation to make the corresponding source code available as required by section 13 of the GNU Affero General Public License.

## Why AGPL

JamRelay is network-oriented software intended to connect clients and AI assistants with external services.

The AGPL licensing model allows the project to remain open source while ensuring that modifications used to provide network-accessible versions of JamRelay remain subject to source-availability obligations defined by the license.

## Historical MIT releases

Earlier versions and commits of JamRelay were published under the MIT License.

Rights validly granted under the MIT License for those versions are not revoked by the later licensing change.

A person who obtained an earlier MIT-licensed version may continue to use that version according to the terms that applied to it.

Later JamRelay versions may contain changes released only under `AGPL-3.0-only`.

The fact that an older version was available under MIT does not automatically make later modifications available under MIT.

## Contributions

Contributions accepted into JamRelay are publicly distributed as part of the project under `AGPL-3.0-only`.

Contributors must also agree to `CLA.md`.

The Contributor License Agreement allows contributors to retain ownership of their work while granting the JamRelay Project Owner sufficient rights to maintain the project and, where legally possible, provide additional licensing options.

The JamRelay Project Owner may separately offer commercial, proprietary, source-available, or other licensing terms for portions of JamRelay for which the Project Owner possesses sufficient rights.

Receiving such a separate license does not invalidate rights granted to users of versions already released under `AGPL-3.0-only`.

Likewise, offering a commercial license does not convert the public AGPL version into proprietary software.

Those licenses continue to apply to the relevant third-party components.

The JamRelay name, logos, domains, visual identity, and other branding may be subject to separate rights.

Forks and modified distributions should clearly distinguish themselves from official JamRelay releases where necessary to avoid confusion.

## No retroactive relicensing

Changes to JamRelay's licensing model apply only where the relevant copyright holders have the legal authority to make those changes.

A later licensing decision does not retroactively cancel licenses that were previously granted for already released versions.
